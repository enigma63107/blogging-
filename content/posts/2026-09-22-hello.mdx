This is the first post. It exists mostly to prove the pipeline works end to
end: a Markdown file in the repo becomes a page, an entry in the archive, a
row in the sitemap and an item in the RSS feed, with no other step in between.

## Why files instead of a CMS

Posts live in `content/posts/` as `.mdx` files. That means every edit is a
commit, every published version is in git history, and there is no database to
back up or admin login to lose. Writing is `git push`.

## What goes here

Whatever I'm working on and whatever I've been thinking about. If a post needs
a diagram or a live demo, MDX lets me drop a component into the prose without
changing anything about how the rest of it works.
